# Bitcoin SV (BSV)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Year** | 2018 |
| **Type** | Hard fork from Bitcoin Cash (permanent chain split) |
| **Status** | Active (separate chain) |

## What it does

Bitcoin SV ("Satoshi's Vision") forked from Bitcoin Cash to pursue even larger blocks. It removed the block size limit entirely, allowing blocks of unlimited size in principle (initially 128 MB, later raised further).

## Why it happened

A dispute within the Bitcoin Cash community over the November 2018 upgrade. One faction (led by Craig Wright and Calvin Ayre) wanted massive block sizes and a return to what they claimed was Satoshi's original design. The other faction (led by Roger Ver and Amaury Sechet) wanted different protocol changes.

## Key details

- Forked from Bitcoin Cash at block 556,766 (November 15, 2018)
- Initial block size: 128 MB (later removed limits entirely)
- Led by Craig Wright, who claims to be Satoshi Nakamoto (a claim rejected by courts and the broader community)
- Has been delisted from several major exchanges

## Outcome

Permanent chain split from Bitcoin Cash (itself a split from Bitcoin). Controversial due to its association with Craig Wright's Satoshi claims. Demonstrates how chain splits can cascade - Bitcoin split into BCH, which split into BSV.
